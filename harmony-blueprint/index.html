Full-Range Advantage

Full-range, so it sounds right everywhere.

Every chord repeats identically across the entire keyboard — bass to lead. That means whatever octave you play in, your melody stays coherent with the harmony underneath. No dead zones, no notes that clash because you played too high or too low. Build your own chords and they get the exact same full-range treatment — not just the pattern library.

Learn By Using It

Understand music theory just by looking at it.

Every pattern shows you the scale degrees and block chords visually — so even if you've never opened a theory book, you start absorbing why these progressions work. It's not a course. It's theory you pick up just by using the tool.
