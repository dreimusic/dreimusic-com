Full-Range Advantage

Full-range, so it sounds right everywhere.

Every chord repeats identically across the entire keyboard — bass to lead. That means whatever octave you play in, your melody stays coherent with the harmony underneath. No dead zones, no notes that clash because you played too high or too low. Build your own chords and they get the exact same full-range treatment — not just the pattern library.

Build Your Own

Build your own chords — or import them.

Don't want to use the pattern library? Click your own chords right into the map — up to 8 — and Harmony Blueprint instantly expands them across the full keyboard, the exact same treatment as every pattern in the library. Already have the chords in a MIDI file? Import it, or press Learn and just play them. No music theory needed. You can't get this wrong.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does this work with my DAW?

Yes — it runs in any DAW that supports VST3 or AU on Mac and Windows, and it's tested and proven in FL Studio, Ableton, Logic and Studio One. If you hit a snag in another DAW, message me and I'll help.

Can I use my own plugins or instruments with Harmony Blueprint?

Yes — just route Harmony Blueprint's MIDI output into your instrument's MIDI input, and you'll hear your own sound instead of Harmony Blueprint's. You're not limited to what's built in — the possibilities are basically endless.

Can I use the chords from my own song?

Yes — drag a MIDI file onto the plugin and it builds the chord map from your file, press Learn and play your chords on your keyboard, or click them in by hand. Up to 8 chords, and they all snap your melody just like the built-in patterns.

Do I need to know music theory to use this?

No — that's the whole point. You'll start understanding it just by using the tool.

Can I use these commercially?

Yes — every pattern is 100% royalty-free with unlimited commercial use. No limits, no additional clearance needed. (The only restriction: you can't resell the MIDI patterns/maps themselves as a standalone product.)

Is this a subscription?

No — one-time purchase, lifetime access to updates.

What if I already have a melody idea?

You can still use Harmony Blueprint reactively — shape your existing idea around proven progressions instead of starting from scratch.
