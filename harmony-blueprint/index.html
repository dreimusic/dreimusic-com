Full-Range Advantage

Full-range, so it sounds right everywhere.

Every chord repeats identically across the entire keyboard — bass to lead. That means whatever octave you play in, your melody stays coherent with the harmony underneath. No dead zones, no notes that clash because you played too high or too low.
